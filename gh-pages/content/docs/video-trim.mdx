Videos preview in the app and can be cut before you save them. The cut is
lossless: FFmpeg copies the existing streams instead of re-encoding, so nothing
is recompressed and a long clip is written in about a second.

## Setting the range

<Steps>

<Step>
### Mark the start
Scrub to where the video should begin and press <Key>[</Key>.
</Step>

<Step>
### Mark the end
Scrub to where it should stop and press <Key>]</Key>.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
<Key>Enter</Key> applies the trim and, if you typed one, the new name — in a
single write.
</Step>

</Steps>

Two ranges are refused rather than silently accepted: one shorter than the
minimum FFmpeg can cut, and one that covers the whole video. In both cases the
app says which it was.

## Where the cut lands

A stream copy cannot split a group of pictures, so the cut lands on the nearest
keyframe rather than exactly on your marker. Expect the real start to sit a
fraction of a second from where you put it. That is the trade for not
re-encoding: every frame that survives is the original frame.

The original is copied into `.quick-media-organizer/trim-backups/` before the cut
is written, which is what <Key>Ctrl+Z</Key> restores from.

Trimming requires FFmpeg. On the Lite build, [install it
first](/docs/install#standard-or-lite).
